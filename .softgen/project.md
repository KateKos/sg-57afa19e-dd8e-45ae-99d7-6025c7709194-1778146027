# Counsel & Chambers

## Vision
A premium legal marketplace connecting businesses with vetted attorneys — positioned against generic SaaS-blue legaltech. Tone: editorial, considered, traditional. The user feels they're hiring counsel from a firm that has stood for a century, not browsing a directory.

Audience: founders, in-house counsel, small-business owners hiring outside counsel for contracts, IP, employment, M&A, litigation.

## Design
Direction: **editorial law-firm gravitas** — 1960s legal stationery, Supreme Court briefs, oxblood leather-bound case books, Roman numerals as section markers, hairline rules, generous margins, classical centered display.

Colors (HSL — keep these keys, change values only if rebranding):
- `--background: 40 33% 94%` (bone cream)
- `--foreground: 220 18% 14%` (ink black)
- `--primary: 354 56% 27%` (oxblood)
- `--accent: 38 45% 48%` (antique gold — hairlines, marks, accents only)
- `--muted: 40 20% 86%` (warm grey)
- `--border: 220 12% 78%`

Fonts:
- Display/Headings: **Newsreader** (high-contrast serif, italic available)
- Body: **Source Serif 4**
- Utility/Case numbers: **JetBrains Mono** (small caps tracking)

Style notes: hairline (1px) rules everywhere, no gradients, no rounded-2xl. Borders subtle (0.5px feel via opacity). Density = airy with classical centered alignment for hero/marketing; left-aligned for content. Roman numerals (I. II. III.) for section sequencing. Small-caps tracked labels (`text-xs uppercase tracking-[0.2em]`).

## Features
- Marketplace landing — find attorneys by practice area
- Attorney directory with profiles, ratings, fixed-fee proposals
- Practice area detail pages (M&A, IP, Employment, etc.)
- "How it works" for clients
- "For attorneys" — application/onboarding for the bar
